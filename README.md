## 1. Downloading APEX BLAZE

1. Download the APEX BLAZE package from the official download link.
2. Save the downloaded file to your computer.
3. If the file is compressed (ZIP), right-click it and select **Extract All**.
4. Insert a USB flash drive or SD card into your computer.
5. Create a folder named **APEX BLAZE** on your storage device.
6. Copy the extracted APEX BLAZE installation files into that folder.
7. Safely eject your USB drive or SD card when the transfer is complete.

## 2. Installing on Your MPC

1. Insert your USB flash drive or SD card into your MPC.
2. Open the MPC browser and navigate to your connected storage device.
3. Locate the **APEX BLAZE** folder.
4. Follow the installation instructions for your specific MPC model and plugin format.
5. Once installed, locate APEX BLAZE in the appropriate plugin or effects menu.
6. Load the plugin and start creating.

**IMPORTANT:** Keep APEX BLAZE's files together in their original folder structure. Do not rename or delete individual plugin files.

## 3. Getting Started

Once APEX BLAZE is loaded, select your desired effects, adjust the controls, and experiment with different combinations to create your own guitar tones.
